Mission

What we are here for

Our mission is to give researchers and technology teams deeper insight from diffraction and total-scattering experiments — turning measured data into reliable structural understanding, with results that are clear, documented and reproducible.

Our expertise spans powder diffraction, total scattering, Pair Distribution Function analysis, structural refinement and atomistic modelling.

Authorship, IP, data ownership and confidentiality

How we handle your work

  • Data ownership. Your data and your results remain yours.
  • Authorship. Where our contribution is scientific rather than routine, authorship or acknowledgement is agreed with you in advance.
  • Intellectual property. Intellectual property arising from your material or question stays with you; scope and terms are agreed in writing for each project.
  • Confidentiality. Data are used only for the agreed project; NDAs are available on request.
