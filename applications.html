From scientific question to the right method

Different materials call for different tools. Here is a sample of the material classes and scientific problems we are usually called in for — and which method answers which question.

Discuss your material system
Functional materials

Ferroelectrics & relaxors

Typical question
What local distortions and correlations sit behind the macroscopic response?
Suitable method
PDF, RMC / big-box modelling, Rietveld
Likely outcome
Local polar structure and its relation to the average structure.
Oxides

Perovskite oxides

Typical question
How do octahedral tilts and cation disorder differ locally and on average?
Suitable method
Rietveld, PDF, variable-temperature series
Likely outcome
Average and local structure of distorted or compositionally complex perovskites.
Ceramics

Functional ceramics

Typical question
Which phases form, and how does structure evolve with composition or processing?
Suitable method
Rietveld, quantitative phase analysis
Likely outcome
Phase fractions, lattice parameters and structure–property trends.
Energy

Energy materials

Typical question
How does structure change during synthesis, cycling or operation?
Suitable method
In-situ / operando PXRD, PDF
Likely outcome
Structural changes tracked against composition, state of charge or temperature.
Nanoscale

Nanomaterials

Typical question
What is the real atomic arrangement when Bragg peaks are broad or weak?
Suitable method
Total scattering, PDF modelling
Likely outcome
Local coordination, finite-size effects and deviations from the ideal structure.
Disorder

Disordered & amorphous materials

Typical question
What short- and medium-range order remains without long-range periodicity?
Suitable method
PDF, RMC, big-box modelling
Likely outcome
Quantitative real-space structure where conventional crystallography fails.
Pharmaceuticals

Pharmaceutical polymorphs

Typical question
Which polymorph or amorphous fraction is present, and does it change?
Suitable method
PXRD phase analysis, PDF where needed
Likely outcome
Polymorph identification and crystallinity tracking.
