FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to commit to anything when I enquire?

No. The enquiry stage is without obligation; we only start a project once you approve the scope.

Can you work with data I have not fully reduced?

Yes — raw or reduced data are both fine. Tell us what you have and we will say what is feasible.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. Data are used only for the agreed project, and NDAs are available on request.

I have no data yet — can you still help?

Yes. We support grant and beamtime proposals and instrument selection. Choose “Project / proposal support” as data type.
