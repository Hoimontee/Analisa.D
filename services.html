We do not sell software; we deliver results, figures and reports tailored to your scientific question. Our work spans routine phase identification to advanced local-structure modelling for complex and disordered materials.

In detail

What each service covers

Powder Diffraction Analysis (PXRD & Rietveld)

Full-pattern analysis of powder diffraction data for phase identification, structural refinement and quantitative interpretation.

  • Phase identification and qualitative analysis
  • Rietveld refinement of average crystal structure
  • Quantitative phase analysis (QPA)
  • Unit-cell and profile parameter analysis
  • Crystallite size and microstrain estimation
  • Variable-temperature and multi-condition datasets

Typical deliverables: processed patterns, fit and difference curves, refined structural and profile parameters, phase fractions, plots and a short technical report.

PDF & Total Scattering

Real-space analysis for materials whose local structure is not captured by average crystallography alone.

  • Total-scattering data processing and validation
  • PDF generation and quality checks
  • Structural modelling in real space
  • Short- and medium-range order
  • Local coordination environments
  • Disorder and local distortions

Typical deliverables: processed total-scattering data, PDF curves, structural models, key distances and coordination numbers, figures and an interpretive report.

Advanced Structural Modelling

Atomistic approaches for complex structures, disorder and local correlations.

  • Reverse Monte Carlo (RMC) and big-box modelling
  • Atomistic models consistent with diffraction and PDF data
  • DFT-informed interpretation of local structure
  • Defects, local symmetry breaking, correlated disorder
  • Comparison of alternative structural models against experiment

Typical deliverables: structural models, model–experiment comparisons, key structural parameters, high-quality figures and a detailed scientific report.

Scientific consulting

Support in developing projects

Powder diffraction and total-scattering methods often have to be planned before any data exist. We can advise on projects in which these experimental methods are essential — from the first idea to the experiment itself.

Grant applicationsDefining the structural-analysis component, methods and expected outcomes of a project.
Beamtime proposalsChoosing technique, facility and measurement strategy, and strengthening the scientific case.
Instrument acquisitionAdvising on requirements and specifications for diffraction and scattering instrumentation.
Support during beamtimeHelp with measurement strategy and analysis while the experiment is running, where needed.

Deliverables and process

For every project you receive

  • Processed data in standard formats.
  • Fits (Rietveld, PDF, etc.) with residuals and quality metrics.
  • Publication-ready figures and tables.
  • A written report explaining results, reliability and scientific implications.

We usually work iteratively: after an initial analysis, we discuss results with you and, where needed, refine the models or extend the analysis.

Typical timelines. Screening: a few working days. Standard analysis: 1–2 weeks. Advanced modelling: 2–4 weeks. Timelines depend on dataset size, complexity and current workload.
