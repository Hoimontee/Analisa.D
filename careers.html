Open position

Analyst — powder diffraction & total scattering

Work on real datasets from researchers and R&D teams: from routine phase analysis and Rietveld refinement to PDF analysis, Data Rescue re-analysis and advanced structural modelling. Choose the way of working that fits your situation.

Full-time

A core analysis role across client projects and Data Rescue work.

  • Take projects from dataset to final report
  • Work across PXRD, total scattering and PDF
  • Help develop methods and workflows

Part-time

Ideal alongside a postdoc, fellowship or other research commitments.

  • A defined weekly or monthly time commitment
  • Projects matched to your expertise and availability
  • The same standards, on a flexible schedule

Freelance

Project-based collaboration on specific datasets or methods.

  • Engagements scoped per project
  • Suited to specialist expertise, e.g. RMC, big-box or DFT-informed modelling
  • Independent work with clear deliverables

What you would work on

Phase identification and quantitative phase analysis
Rietveld refinement of powder diffraction data
Total-scattering data reduction and PDF analysis
Re-analysis of previously collected datasets (Data Rescue)
Structural modelling — real-space refinement, RMC / big-box, DFT-informed interpretation
Clear technical reports and publication-quality figures

Who we're looking for

Early-career scientists are welcome.

We would like to hear from young PhDs and postdocs in materials science, solid-state chemistry, physics, mineralogy or related fields who enjoy getting the most out of diffraction and scattering data. Depth in one or two of the areas on the right matters more than covering everything.

What we ask for

A PhD (completed or close to completion) or postdoctoral experience in a relevant field
Hands-on experience analysing diffraction or scattering data
Careful, reproducible working habits and clear scientific writing
Comfort handling confidential client data

Expertise we value

Powder XRD Rietveld refinement Synchrotron & neutron data Total scattering PDF analysis RMC / big-box modelling DFT Complex oxides Ferroelectric materials Energy materials Nanomaterials Python scripting

Familiarity with tools such as TOPAS, GSAS-II, FullProf, PDFgui, DiffPy-CMI or RMCProfile is a plus.
