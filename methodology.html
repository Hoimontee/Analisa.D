The goal is to answer the scientific question — not merely to fit peaks

Many materials contain disorder, nanoscale effects and local distortions that stay hidden in an average crystallographic model. We combine reciprocal-space and real-space methods to investigate materials across length scales, and we report what the data can and cannot support.

Quality

Validation, uncertainty and reproducibility

Validation

Data are checked before they are modelled — normalisation, corrections and instrument resolution — and fits are judged by residuals and physical plausibility, not by a single agreement factor.

Uncertainty

Refined values are reported with their uncertainties, and we state which parameters are well determined by the data and which are not.

Competing models

Where more than one structural picture is possible, alternatives are compared against the same data rather than a single model being assumed.

Reproducibility

Processing steps, models and key parameters are documented so that the analysis can be repeated, checked and extended — by you or by us.

Experience

Instruments and facilities

We work with data from synchrotron and neutron facilities and from laboratory diffractometers, in the formats common to these instruments. If your data come from a specific beamline or instrument, tell us in your enquiry and we will confirm that we can handle them.
