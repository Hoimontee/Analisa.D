01 — The idea

We infer structural pattern from raw diffraction data.

Every analysis starts the same way: counts on a detector, turned into a structural answer through careful reduction, refinement, and modeling.

02 — Case

Nanoparticles

Sub-10 nm particles broaden and weaken Bragg peaks. Total scattering recovers coordination environments conventional refinement can't reach.

03 — Case

Pharmaceutical molecules

Dense, low-symmetry powder patterns from organic crystals — used for polymorph identification and crystallinity tracking.

04 — Case

Single crystal

Discrete reflections index directly to a unit cell — the average structure resolved at its most precise.

05 — Case

Amorphous solids

No Bragg peaks at all. PDF is often the only quantitative route to short- and medium-range structure.

06 — Case

Battery electrodes

Cycling drives local disorder that average-structure refinement misses. PDF tracks it directly — pristine vs. cycled.

What we do

Reciprocal-space and real-space methods, matched to your question

We combine reciprocal-space and real-space methods to answer specific structural questions.

Powder diffraction & Rietveld analysis

Phase identification, quantitative phase analysis and full-pattern refinement for average crystal structure.

PDF & total scattering

Real-space analysis of local structure, coordination environments and short- to medium-range order.

Advanced structural modelling

Reverse Monte Carlo, big-box modelling and DFT-informed interpretation for disordered and complex materials.

Data Rescue & consulting

Re-analysis of old or unused datasets, plus support for proposals, beamtime planning and instrument selection.

Process

How a project typically runs

01

Send your data and question

You share raw or reduced diffraction / total-scattering data and describe the scientific problem.

02

Assessment and proposal

We review data quality, discuss feasible analysis routes, and propose a scope, timeline and quotation.

03

Analysis and modelling

We process the data, perform refinements and/or structural modelling, and validate the results.

04

Deliverables and interpretation

You receive processed data, fits, figures, tables, and a report explaining the structural conclusions and their limitations.
